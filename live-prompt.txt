Preserve existing useful functionality while correcting errors, completing missing features, and professionally enhancing the entire application.**

# 19. CRITICAL DEVELOPMENT RULES

Follow these requirements throughout development.

**1. Preserve existing functionality.** Do not remove useful features, existing business workflows, or established design elements unnecessarily.

**2. Do not build a frontend-only application.** Every major module must connect to the backend and persistent database.

**3. Do not use Local Storage as the production database.** Critical business records must be persisted in the database.

**4. Do not hardcode dashboard statistics.** All figures must be calculated from actual database records.

**5. Do not create non-functional placeholder pages.** Every implemented navigation item must open a properly developed module.

**6. Do not hardcode business branding.** Business logos, colours, descriptions, and settings must be dynamically configurable.

**7. Do not compromise financial integrity.** Payments, transactions, invoices, refunds, and adjustments must be traceable.

**8. Do not compromise stock accuracy.** Every confirmed sale, purchase, return, adjustment, and transfer must update the appropriate inventory records.

**9. Do not force online payments.** Cash and authorised manual payment methods must always remain available independently of payment gateway availability.

**10. Do not expose API credentials.** Payment, SMS, email, and eFRIS secrets must remain secure on the backend.

**11. Do not mix business data.** One business must never be able to access another business's private records.

**12. Do not build for desktop alone.** All interfaces must be responsive.

**13. Do not invent official eFRIS integration details.** Use genuine URA specifications and credentials when supplied.

**14. Do not claim an integration is live without testing it.** Clearly distinguish configured, tested, and operational integrations.

**15. Do not delete completed financial records silently.** Use controlled reversals, refunds, adjustments, and audit trails.

**16. Do not introduce unnecessary dependencies.** Keep the system maintainable and secure.

**17. Do not ignore existing errors.** Investigate and fix the root causes rather than hiding warnings or suppressing errors.

**18. Do not sacrifice compatibility.** Ensure the existing XAMPP development environment works while maintaining a realistic PostgreSQL migration path.

**19. Do not stop at generating code.** Run the application, test its workflows, fix errors, and verify the actual implementation.

**20. Do not claim 100% production readiness without evidence.** Verify the actual functionality, security, integrations, deployment configuration, and test results.
